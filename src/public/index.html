      Scam safety · Before you pay checklist
      • Never pay a reservation fee or deposit before visiting in person. Avoid GCash or bank transfers just to "hold" a room.
      • Inspect the room and meet the caretaker on-site. Check door/window locks, water supply, sub-meter readings, and street flood history.
      • Confirm what is included in writing. Under Philippine rent control (RA 9653, rent ≤ ₱10,000), landlords may ask at most 1 month advance and 2 months deposit.
      • Watch for red flags: "Too good to be true" aircon studios at ₱1,000–₱2,000, pressure to pay immediately, or refusal to allow an in-person viewing.
